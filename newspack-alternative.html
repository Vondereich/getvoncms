Newspack or a self-hosted news CMS?

Newspack is a managed WordPress platform built for news organizations. VonCMS is free software you run on your own hosting. They solve the same problem in very different ways.

Two different models

Newspack: managed service

Automattic hosts and maintains a WordPress setup tuned for news, with support and reader revenue tools. You pay a monthly fee and focus on journalism.

VonCMS: self-hosted software

You download a free publishing CMS and run it on your own PHP and MySQL hosting. You pay only for hosting, and you are responsible for updates and backups.

Neither is better for everyone. The right choice depends on your budget, your technical help, and the features your business model needs.

What each costs

Newspack's pricing page lists plans by annual publisher revenue, starting at US$750 per month for publications under US$300,000 a year, with higher tiers above that. Check the current page before you decide, because pricing can change.

VonCMS software costs nothing. Your costs are a domain, hosting, and any help or optional services you choose. Many small publications run on shared or VPS hosting that costs far less than a managed platform, but the time you spend maintaining it is a real cost too.

Choose Newspack if…

  • Your budget covers the monthly fee and you want someone else to handle hosting, security, and updates.
  • Donations, memberships, or subscriptions are central to your revenue and you want them supported out of the box.
  • You value a support team and a community of other Newspack newsrooms.

Choose VonCMS if…

  • You are starting small and the monthly fee of a managed platform does not fit your budget.
  • You want your publication on hosting you control, with direct access to your database and source.
  • You or someone you work with can handle installation, backups, and updates.
  • Your priority is a clear writer-to-editor workflow and a public news site, rather than a full reader revenue stack.
