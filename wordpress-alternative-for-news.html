Guide

A WordPress alternative for news websites.

WordPress can run almost any kind of website. If yours is a news publication, a smaller CMS built around the newsroom may need less setup. Here is an honest comparison to help you choose.

The short answer

WordPress has a mature ecosystem, thousands of themes and plugins, and a large pool of developers. It remains a sensible choice for many publishers.

VonCMS is a smaller, publishing-focused alternative. It ships the parts a news website usually needs, including writer submissions, staff review, scheduling, SEO output, and bundled news themes, in one application. It runs on the same kind of PHP and MySQL hosting as WordPress.

Choose the CMS that fits your publication and the setup you are willing to maintain.

Features can depend on version, plan, plugins, and configuration. Check your real workflow before you move. See the full comparison with other CMSs.

Stay with WordPress if…

  • You depend on specific plugins, such as a paywall, membership system, or ad stack, with no VonCMS equivalent you have tested.
  • Your developer or agency already maintains WordPress for you.
  • You need a large marketplace of themes and integrations.
  • You run several kinds of websites, not only a publication.

Consider VonCMS if…

  • You want writer submissions and staff review without assembling plugins.
  • You prefer fewer moving parts: one application with publishing, SEO, themes, comments, newsletter tools, and analytics included.
  • You want to stay on ordinary PHP and MySQL hosting that you control.
  • You are comfortable with a smaller ecosystem and want to read or change the source.

Migrating from WordPress

VonCMS includes a WordPress XML importer. Treat the move as a project, not a button click:

  1. Export your content from WordPress as an XML file.
  2. Install VonCMS on a staging domain and run the import there first.
  3. Check articles, categories, tags, images, embeds, excerpts, and internal links. Password-protected items come in as Drafts with a warning.
  4. Plan redirects if any public URLs will change.
  5. Keep the original site and export until every check passes.

WordPress themes and plugins are not VonCMS extensions, so recreate the features you rely on. See media and WordPress import.

Other WordPress alternatives

VonCMS is not the only option. Ghost focuses on publications, newsletters, and memberships, and runs on Node.js or its managed service. Statamic and Grav are PHP-based CMSs with different content models. Strapi and Payload are headless systems that need a frontend you build. The homepage comparison summarizes each.

If you are weighing a managed news platform instead, read Newspack vs a self-hosted CMS or the enterprise news CMS comparison.

Next steps

The best test is your own workflow. Install VonCMS on a test domain, import a sample of your WordPress content, and run a week of stories through review.
