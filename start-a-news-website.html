How to start a news website on your own hosting.

A practical path from idea to first published story: plan your coverage, choose hosting, install a publishing CMS, add your team, and keep the site running.

1. Plan your coverage before your software

The CMS matters less than knowing what you will publish. Before installing anything, write down:

  • Your beat. A town, a region, an industry, or a community. A focused beat is easier to cover well and easier for readers to find.
  • Your sections. Three to six categories is a good start, for example News, Politics, Business, Sport, and Opinion. You can add more later.
  • Your team. Who writes, who edits, and who decides when a story goes live. Even a two-person newsroom benefits from a second read before publication.
  • Your publishing rhythm. Daily, weekly, or as stories break. A steady rhythm matters more than volume.

2. Choose a domain and hosting

Register a short domain that matches your publication's name. Then choose hosting. A self-hosted news website gives you control over your content and database, but your publication is responsible for keeping it running.

VonCMS runs on ordinary PHP and MySQL hosting, including many shared hosting plans:

Hosting checklistVonCMS runtime
PHP8.2 minimum; 8.3 or newer recommended, with the DOM extension enabled.
DatabaseMySQL 5.7 or newer, or a compatible MariaDB.
Web serverApache or LiteSpeed with .htaccess support. Nginx needs equivalent rewrite rules.
Not neededNode.js, npm, or a separate frontend server.
HTTPSUse an SSL certificate from your host. Most hosts include one.

Ask your host whether these are available before you buy. See the full hosting requirements.

3. Install the CMS

With VonCMS, installation uses the ready-to-install package, called the Deploy ZIP in each release:

  1. Download the latest Deploy ZIP from GitHub Releases.
  2. Extract it into your hosting web root, such as public_html/.
  3. Create a MySQL database and database user in your hosting control panel.
  4. Open https://yourdomain.com/install. Read the setup key from data/install_setup.key using your host's File Manager or SFTP, and enter it in the installer.
  5. Connect the database and create your admin account.

The installation guide covers each step in more detail.

4. Set up the site

Sign in at /admin and work through the basics before you publish:

  1. Set your site name, tagline, and the exact public HTTPS address under Settings > General > Domain URL.
  2. Choose one of the bundled themes, then upload your logo and favicon.
  3. Create your categories and navigation menu. Add About and Contact pages, so readers know who is behind the publication.
  4. Publish a test article and check it on a phone and a desktop.

Moving from an existing WordPress site? VonCMS includes a WordPress XML importer. Read how to migrate from WordPress before you switch.

5. Add your team and a review step

A news website needs more than a single login. Give each person an account with the access they need:

Writers

Draft stories and submit them for review. They cannot publish on their own.

Moderators and Admins

Review submissions, then return them for changes, schedule them, or publish them.

This keeps a second pair of eyes on every story. Writers can withdraw a submission, revise it, and submit it again. See the editorial workflow.

6. Help readers and search engines find you

  • Write a clear headline, an excerpt, and a meta description for each story.
  • Check your public /sitemap.xml and /robots.txt, then submit the sitemap in Google Search Console.
  • For time-sensitive reporting, consider the optional News Sitemap. It helps discovery but does not guarantee Google News inclusion.
  • Share stories through your social channels and newsletter. Search traffic for a new site usually takes months to build.

VonCMS outputs metadata, canonical URLs, structured data, sitemaps, and feeds by default. Rankings still depend on your reporting and your site.

7. Keep it running

Self-hosting means you own the routine work. Put these on a calendar:

  • Backups: keep external copies of your database, configuration, and uploads. Test a restore at least once.
  • Updates: back up first, then follow the upgrade guide for each release.
  • PHP version: updating the CMS does not update PHP. Keep your hosting on a supported version.

If you would rather not handle this yourself, arrange help from a developer or hosting provider.

What it costs

VonCMS is free, open source software under GPL-3.0-only. Your running costs are:

  • A domain name, renewed every year.
  • PHP and MySQL hosting, paid monthly or yearly.
  • Optional services you choose, such as email delivery, an AI provider key, or developer help.

Comparing this with a managed platform? Read Newspack vs a self-hosted CMS.
